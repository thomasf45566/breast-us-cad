- `src/train.py:118-131` builds loaders from explicit `train_folds`/`val_folds`;
  `src/data.py:make_dataloaders` raises on overlap; `pos_weight` is computed from train
  folds only (`train.py:134-136`). `src/cross_validate.py:60-69` passes
  `train_folds=[f for f in ALL_FOLDS if f != k]`. All v1 configs have
  `data.root: data/raw/busbra`; no v1 script reads `data/raw/{breast_poland,busi,
  gdph_sysucc}` (grep: only `external_val.py`, `dedup_busi.py`, `phash_sweep.py`,
  `birads_comparison.py`, `v2_*`). BiomedCLIP init is used only for v2 (Q2).
- **Checkpoint provenance (wandb, local `wandb/run-*/files/config.yaml` +
  `wandb-metadata.json`):** the five `cv_vit` runs (c9adedd pre-rewrite hash,
  2026-08-28 00:44–08:42 UTC) record `train_folds`/`val_folds` =
  [2,3,4,5]/[1], [1,3,4,5]/[2], [1,2,4,5]/[3], [1,2,3,5]/[4], [1,2,3,4]/[5] and
  `best_val_auc` 0.95256/0.94114/0.91929/0.91320/0.92720 = `reports/cv_vit_summary.csv`.
  Fold 5 was therefore excluded from the fold-5 checkpoint's training. The other
  four frozen members were trained on fold 5 (by design; RESULTS.md:170 discloses
  that the ensemble has no unbiased internal estimate).
- **Misleading embedded provenance:** every `models/cv_vit_fold{k}.pt` stores
  `config.data.train_folds=[1,2,3,4], val_folds=[5]` regardless of k
  (`train.py:196` saves the raw YAML, not the folds actually used). Anyone auditing
  from the checkpoint alone would conclude all five were trained on folds 1–4.
- **Epoch-selection bias (not leakage, but it inflates the internal headline):**
  `train.py:191-199` saves the checkpoint at the epoch with the best AUC on the
  validation fold, and `cross_validate.py:72` then reports AUC on that same fold. The
  per-fold AUCs in RESULTS.md (0.9307 ± 0.0161) are literally the max-over-30-epochs
  of the held-out fold's AUC (wandb `best_val_auc` == summary `auc` to 16 digits).
  The pooled OOF used for T and the threshold inherits this. Nowhere is this stated.

**Verdict: PASS on patient-level split integrity (no patient crosses folds; no external
image reaches v1 training; provenance corroborated by wandb). CONCERN — major — for the
best-epoch-on-the-same-fold selection that optimistically biases every internal
number, and minor for the fold-file location and checkpoint-config misstatements.**
